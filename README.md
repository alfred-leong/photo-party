# photo-party

One-off photo slideshow for a gathering.

- Open https://alfred-leong.github.io/photo-party/ on the screen device (host).
- Guests scan the QR code and upload one photo each.
- Photos are encrypted in the guest's browser (key is in the QR link) and relayed through ntfy.sh (free, no account); they expire there after ~3 hours.
- Press Start: photos are shuffled and shown for 30 seconds each.
